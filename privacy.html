Privacy Policy

Come and Hear publishing tools · effective November 6, 2026

This policy covers the private production tools of the YouTube channel Come and Hear (the "tools"). The tools use YouTube API Services. By using them you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to the data Google handles.

Who uses the tools

Only the owner of the Come and Hear channel. The tools are not offered to the public and no other person signs in to them.

What data the tools access

With the owner's authorization through Google sign-in, the tools access, for the Come and Hear channel only:

  • the channel's videos, playlists, captions, thumbnails and their titles, descriptions, tags and translations (YouTube Data API), to publish and update them;
  • the channel's aggregated statistics, such as views, watch time and audience retention (YouTube Analytics API), to plan new videos.

The tools do not collect personal information about viewers, commenters or any other YouTube user.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to produce and manage the channel's own videos. The authorization token is stored in a file readable only by the owner, on the owner's private server. Video metadata and aggregated statistics are kept in the owner's private project records for as long as they are useful for the channel and are deleted when no longer needed. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

The access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page, myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the stored token stops working and is deleted.
