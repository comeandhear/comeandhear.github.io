Come and Hear

Faithful Bible stories for adults, told by an elder at a campfire.

Come and Hear is a YouTube channel that retells the stories of the Bible (King James Version) for adults, with narration, images and on-screen Scripture. Watch it at youtube.com/channel/UCRrlLKF5GnbN8nMmjt1ilhw.

Come and Hear publishing tools

The channel uses a private set of production tools, run by the channel owner on their own server, to manage the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API and to read the channel's own statistics through the YouTube Analytics API. With the owner's authorization, the tools:

  • set the title, description, tags and translated titles and descriptions of the channel's videos;
  • upload and update the channel's captions and thumbnails, and add videos to the channel's playlists;
  • read the channel's own video statistics (views, watch time, retention) to plan new videos.

The tools are not offered to the public: the only account that signs in is the channel's own. They use YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service.
